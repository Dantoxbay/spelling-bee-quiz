Privacy Policy – Spelling Bee Quiz

Developer: Dantoxbay Apps · Last updated: November 26, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Spelling Bee Quiz (package name com.dantoxbay.spellingbeequiz), published on Google Play by Dantoxbay Apps ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files.

The app saves your settings (such as level, voice and accent) and your game progress only on your own device, using the app's local storage. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Text-to-speech

To read words aloud, the app uses built-in recordings and the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (for example, Speech Services by Google). Only the word being spoken is passed to that engine on your device. The engine is provided by its own developer and is covered by that developer's privacy policy.

3. Internet use and third-party services

The app works offline. When an internet connection is available, it may load a typeface from Google Fonts. As with any website or app that loads a font, Google receives standard technical information such as your device's IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

Versions of the app that show ads use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device type and general usage and diagnostic information, to show ads, measure them, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud. Depending on your settings and location, ads may be personalised.

  • Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive any information from AdMob that directly identifies you.

5. Sharing of information

We do not collect personal information, so we do not share or sell it. Data handled by the third-party services above (Google Fonts and Google AdMob) is shared with Google under Google's own privacy policy.

6. Data security and retention

Information saved by the app stays on your device. Data processed by third-party services is kept according to those providers' policies. Any data sent over the internet is protected with encryption (HTTPS).

7. Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your choices

  • Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to delete everything it saved on your device.
  • Manage ad personalisation in your device's Google ad settings, as described above.
  • Contact us with any question or request about your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when new features are added. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
